## Frequently asked questions

### What font does Cloudflare use?

Cloudflare uses FT Kunst Grotesk across its interface, for both headings and body text.

### What are Cloudflare's brand colors?

Cloudflare's core brand colors include #ff5e1f (primary / brand), #ffffff (text on primary), #ffffff (background), #f0f0f0 (surface). The full token table above lists every color with its role.

### Does Cloudflare publish a public design system?

This page publishes Cloudflare's design tokens as a ready to copy DESIGN.md: the color, typography, and spacing values above are structured so you can paste them into Claude, Cursor, or Windsurf and have an AI agent build in Cloudflare's style.
